| | |
|---|---|
| **What it is** | A checklist that turns the twelve modules into 36 questions a practice can score by evidence |
| **Who it is for** | A practice owner or privacy officer who wants to see where the paper and the practice are far apart |
| **Time** | About 45 minutes the first time, if the documents are to hand |
| **You get** | A printable checklist with a scoring sheet, and Clinic X's baseline scored question by question |

## 1. How to use it

Go through the 36 questions. For each, answer **Yes**, **Partly**, **No** or **Not applicable**. Count 2, 1 and 0 points, leave out the questions that do not apply, and divide the points by the maximum.

- **Score by evidence, not by intention.** If you cannot show it in two minutes (a document, a register entry, a dated record), it is not a Yes.
- **Partly** means it exists but is incomplete, untested, undated, or not yet used in practice.
- **Not applicable** is for questions that do not fit your practice (for example, you have no cameras and no employees). Leave it out of the total.
- **A score is not compliance.** It shows where the paper and the practice are far apart. Compliance depends on your facts and your national law.

## 2. What the score means

| Share of the maximum | What it usually means | Where to start |
|---|---|---|
| Below 40% | Most of the programme is missing or only an idea | Modules 1, 3, 5 and 6 first: know what you process, answer requests, handle incidents |
| 40% to 70% | Documents exist, but much is untested or incomplete | Practise what you have: training (8), drills (6), provider reviews (9), restore tests (7) |
| Above 70% | Most things exist and have been used | Close the open items, re-score each year, and look at DPIAs (12) and cameras (10) |

The score is meant for the owner of the programme, not for an audience. The most useful output is the list of questions answered "No" or "Partly", each with an owner and a date.

## 3. Clinic X at 7 December 2026

Clinic X scored itself on the day its programme was complete on paper: the documents were adopted, almost nothing had yet been practised. That is why the total is modest, and why the training area scores zero: the first session is on 19 January 2027.

**Reading it.** Clinic X scores 47 of 70, which is 67%, in the middle band. Areas 1, 2 and 12 are full because they are documents. Training (8) is zero because nothing has been delivered. Incidents (6), security (7) and providers (9) are held back by things that have not yet been tested: a drill, a restore from backup, the provider reviews. Each of those has a date in the plan.

Re-scored a year later, the same questions would show what changed. That is the point of repeating it.

## 5. Limits

- **A score is not compliance.** It measures whether documents and records exist and have been used, not whether they are right.
- **The questions follow this programme.** A practice with other risks (telemedicine, research, a large website) will need more questions.
- **The weights are equal.** A "No" on incidents and a "No" on the notice count the same. In practice they do not carry the same risk.
- **The Clinic X answers are fictional,** and consistent with the dates and registers in the modules.
