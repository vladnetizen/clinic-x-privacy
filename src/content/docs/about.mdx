> **Fictional example.** Clinic X and everyone in it are invented. This site is educational and is not legal advice.

## Why it exists

Privacy documents are easy to find and hard to judge. A template shows a form; it does not show how the notice, the record of processing, the retention schedule and the incident procedure fit together, or what a proportionate version looks like for a practice with three or four people.

This case study follows one invented clinic from a single notice to a working programme, so that the whole chain can be read, checked and criticised.

## Who made it

<Author />

The documents were drafted with the help of an AI assistant and edited by the author, using sources read on the dates the pages give. The assistant can make mistakes, which is one reason the pages separate what was checked from what was not. The [methodology](../13-methodology/) explains the labels and lists every open item.

## Using it

- **Documents and page text:** proposed for reuse under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Credit the case study and keep the disclaimer.
- **Code and data files:** MIT licence.
- **Mistakes:** if you find one, please open an issue in the repository.

> **Educational example, not legal advice.** The documents show one proportionate approach and may be wrong for your practice or your country. Check current law and your own facts, and take legal advice before using any document with real patients, staff or providers.
