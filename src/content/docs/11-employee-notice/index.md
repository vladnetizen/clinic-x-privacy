## 1. Why it exists

The patient notice does not cover the people who work in the practice. They are data subjects too, with their own data: ID numbers, pay, bank accounts, sick leave, qualifications. The practice also films them, in the sense that the cameras described in [module 10](../10-cctv-lia/) record the places where they work. Art. 13 GDPR requires the practice to tell them what it does with all of it, and Art. 5 of Law no. 190/2018 requires prior, complete and explicit information before employees are monitored.

Three choices shape the notice:

- **It starts with six lines.** A box at the top says, in plain words, what matters most. Staff who read nothing else still know about the cameras, the rights and the complaint route.
- **It does not ask for consent.** Employees are seldom free to refuse, so consent from them is weak. The notice says which basis applies to each purpose: the contract, a legal duty, or legitimate interest.
- **It says the uncomfortable thing.** The cameras were there before the programme. The notice states that, and what the practice did about it.

## 2. What the notice says

The summary box at the top of the notice:

- We keep the data we need to employ you, pay you and meet our legal duties. Nothing more.
- We do not ask for your consent for this. The law and your contract are the reasons, and consent from an employee would not be free.
- Four cameras cover the common areas. We do not use the images to check your work, your hours or your conduct. Only Dr. A can look at them. They are deleted after 7 days.
- Your data stays in the EU. We make no automated decisions about you.
- You can ask to see, correct or limit your data, and you can object to the cameras. Using your rights does not affect your job.
- You may complain to the authority (ANSPDCP) at any time, without going through us.

The data it covers:

- Identification: name, national ID number (CNP), identity document, date and place of birth, citizenship.
- Contact: address, phone, e-mail.
- The working relationship: role, contract, pay, working time, leave, appraisals, sanctions, training done.
- Qualifications: studies, diplomas, licence to practise, professional authorisations, professional liability insurance.
- Financial: bank account, income, deductions.
- Dependants, for deductions or leave, if you give us the information.
- Health, only as the law requires: the occupational health certificate and sick leave certificates.
- Use of our systems: user accounts and access logs of the practice's applications.
- Video images, from the cameras in the common areas (section 5).

And the purposes, bases and retention. Each row ties to a processing activity in the [record of processing](../03-ropa/) (PA-11, PA-07 and PA-12) and to a row of the [retention schedule](../04-retention/):

| Purpose | Legal basis | Data | Kept for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Making and running your contract; pay | Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) | Identification, contact, contract, pay, bank account | Personnel file: while you work here and the period set by archiving law after (RET-11(a)). Payroll statements: 5 years (RET-11(b)) |
| Legal duties: employee register, tax and social filings, reports to authorities | Art. 6(1)(c). National ID number: Art. 4 of Law no. 190/2018 | Identification, CNP, income | As above |
| Health and safety at work, occupational health, sick leave | Art. 6(1)(c); Art. 9(2)(b) (employment and social security law) | Occupational health certificate, sick leave certificates | As above |
| Working time and leave records | Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) | Hours, attendance, leave | As above |
| Checking qualifications and the right to practise | Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) | Diplomas, certificates, authorisations | As above |
| Safety of people and property: video cameras | Art. 6(1)(f), after a written assessment; Art. 5 of Law no. 190/2018 | Video images | 7 days, never more than 30 (RET-06) |
| Security of information: accounts and access logs | Art. 6(1)(c) (Art. 32 GDPR) and (f) | User name, access logs | A period to be set with the software provider by 31 March 2027; until then as the software keeps them |
| Training and proof of training | Art. 6(1)(c) and (f) | Name, signature, topics | With the personnel file (RET-11(a)) |
| Establishing, exercising or defending legal claims, if needed | Art. 6(1)(f); Art. 9(2)(f) | The data the case needs | While the case lasts and the legal time limits run |

How long we keep data. We keep data no longer than needed. The criteria are: the personnel file and contract, while you work here and then the periods in employment and archiving law; payroll statements and accounting documents, the periods in accounting and tax law; video images, as above. The exact periods are available on request. When they end, data is deleted or destroyed securely unless the law requires it to be kept.

Recipients:

- Dr. A as the manager of the practice, and only as far as the role requires.
- Public authorities, as the law requires: the labour inspectorate and the general register of employees (REGES), the tax authority, the pension and health insurance funds, the professional college.
- Providers who help us, under contract (processors): the accounting firm, for payroll; the providers of the practice software and of IT support, for technical access; the CCTV installer, only if it must reach the recorder.
- The occupational health provider, which acts as a separate controller for medical checks; the bank that pays your salary.

We do not sell your data. We do not transfer it outside the EU/EEA. We make no decisions about you by automated means only, and we do not profile you.

### The camera section

This is the part Art. 5 of Law no. 190/2018 cares about most. It must match what the [LIA](../10-cctv-lia/) says, to the letter.

- Areas: only common areas: the entrance vestibule, reception, the waiting room and the corridor to the rooms. There is no camera in consultation rooms, the treatment room, the toilet or the staff room. No sound is recorded.
- Purposes: the safety of patients and staff, the protection of property and the investigation of incidents. The images are not used to assess your work, your hours or your conduct, and nobody watches them live. If an image shows an incident covered by the purposes (for example a theft or an assault), it can be used to deal with that incident, including with the police.
- Basis: the practice's legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), after a written assessment that also weighs your rights. The cameras were already in place when this notice was prepared. The practice has since assessed them, consulted you and fixed these rules.
- Retention: 7 days, then automatic overwriting, unless the images are needed for an incident or requested by an authority. Never more than 30 days.
- Access: Dr. A only, with an individual account. Every viewing beyond the monthly check, and every export, is logged. Authorities may receive images on a written request that states the legal basis.
- Consultation: there is no union or employee representative, so Dr. A consulted both employees directly on Thursday 3 December 2026; the record is kept.
- Your rights: including access to images of you, and objection (section 7). Ask Dr. A.

### Same facts, five places

The camera rules appear in five documents. A mismatch between any two is a finding. This table is the check made on 7 December 2026, when the LIA was approved.

| Fact | Employee notice | Patient notice, section 7 | Record of processing, PA-07 | LIA | Retention schedule |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Areas | Entrance vestibule, reception, waiting room, corridor | Same four, named | Common areas | C1 to C4 | n/a |
| No camera in | Rooms where care is given, toilet, staff room | Consultation rooms, treatment room, toilets | Same, plus the staff room | Same | n/a |
| Sound | None | None | None | None | n/a |
| Retention | 7 days; never more than 30 | 7 days | 7 days; never more than 30 | 7 days; never more than 30 | RET-06: 7 days; never more than 30 |
| Who can view | Dr. A only | "Authorised staff" | Dr. A only | Dr. A only | n/a |
| Use for performance | Not used | Not used | Not stated | Not used (G13) | n/a |
| Basis | Legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f), and Art. 5 of Law no. 190/2018 | Art. 6(1)(f) | Art. 6(1)(f) | Art. 6(1)(f) | n/a |

**One difference found.** The patient notice says access is for "authorised staff", while the other documents say Dr. A only. It is not wrong, since "authorised staff" currently means one person, but it will drift if a second person is ever authorised. The two should be reworded together at the next revision of the patient notice. Nothing else disagrees.

**A timing point.** The notice is dated 3 December and refers to a written assessment that Dr. A approved on 7 December. The assessment was in draft at the meeting, and the meeting's questions went into the final text. Ideally the approved assessment would come first. Here the consultation had to inform it, and the notice would have to be re-issued if the final assessment had changed the rules. It did not.

## 5. Joiner and leaver checklist

The checklist follows a person's data from before they start until the retention periods end. It is also the "checklist to be created" in [control SEC-31](../07-security-policy/), which stays "Partial" in the controls register until it has been used for a real joiner or leaver.

| Stage | What is done with the data | Document or control | Who | When |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Before the first day | Ask only for what the contract and the law need: identity, CNP for payroll, bank account, qualifications. Do not ask for health data, other than the occupational health certificate, through the provider. File everything in the personnel file. | Employee notice; RET-11(a) | Dr. A | Before the contract is signed |
| Before access to any system | The notice is given and acknowledged. The confidentiality undertaking is signed. The 45-minute session is held. An individual account is created with the least access the role needs. | Annex 1; Staff Pack undertaking (SEC-29); training plan (SEC-30) | Dr. A | Before the account is switched on |
| First day | Show the camera areas and the notice at reception. Hand over the Staff Pack and keys. Note the keys in the key list. | Staff Pack; key list (SEC-18) | Dr. A | First day |
| During the job | Keep the file correct. Update the notice when processing changes and give the new version. Hold the annual session. Review access rights. | Training plan; access review (module 7) | Dr. A | Each December; access review each June |
| When someone leaves | Switch off accounts the same day. Collect keys, devices and papers. Remind the person that confidentiality continues. Close the personnel file and note the date: the retention periods start. | Security policy 4 (joining and leaving); SEC-05, SEC-31; RET-11(a) and (b) | Dr. A | Last day |
| After they leave | Keep the file and payroll records for the legal periods. Destroy them when the periods end and note it in the destruction register. | Retention schedule; destruction register | Dr. A | When each period ends |
| Candidates who are not hired | Delete the CV and notes 6 months after the process ends, or 12 months if the person agreed that you keep it. | RET-12 | Receptionist D | Each January and July |

## 6. What was verified, and what was not

**Checked**

- **Art. 5 of Law no. 190/2018.** Read on 7 November 2026 in a published copy (see [module 10](../10-cctv-lia/)): prior, complete and explicit information of employees is one of five conditions for monitoring by video.
- **WP29 Opinion 2/2017 on data processing at work,** adopted 8 June 2017, read in a [published version](https://gdpr-text.com/en/guidelines/dataprocessingatwork/). It says employees are seldom in a position to freely give, refuse or revoke consent, that a legitimate interest alone does not override employees' rights, that processing at work should be done in the least intrusive way possible, and that employees should be clearly and fully informed.
- **EDPB Guidelines 3/2019** (read in module 10): employers should in most cases not rely on consent for video surveillance of employees.
- **Art. 13 and 14 GDPR.** The content of the notice follows the list in Art. 13 (identity and contact of the controller, purposes and bases, legitimate interests pursued, recipients, transfers, retention criteria, rights, the right to complain, whether providing data is required, automated decisions). Art. 14 applies to data that does not come from the person, such as data from authorities.

**Not verified, to be confirmed**

- **Retention of personnel files.** The period set by Romanian employment and archiving law is not confirmed (RET-11(a)). The notice says "the period set by archiving law" without a number for that reason.
- **Art. 4 of Law no. 190/2018 (national ID number).** A summary read on 7 November 2026 lists safeguards that include regular staff training. The exact wording is still to be confirmed. The notice cites the article only as the basis for processing the number.
- **The general register of employees (REGES) and the labour inspectorate.** The names and the duties are taken from the Romanian original of this notice and were not re-checked.
- **Occupational health.** The basis for processing the certificate (Art. 9(2)(b)) and the provider's role as a separate controller are assumptions to check with a lawyer.
- **How long access logs are kept.** No period is set yet. The notice says so and gives the date (31 March 2027) by which the period will be fixed with the software provider.
- **Whether to treat the visiting physician as a collaborator or as an independent professional** for notice purposes. The page gives him the notice and the acknowledgement either way.

## 7. Design decisions

**1. A summary box before the legal text.** Six lines, in the second person. The full notice is there for the inspector. The box is there for the person.

**2. No consent for employment data.** Consent is the wrong basis between employer and employee. The notice names a contract, a law or a legitimate interest for each row, and the acknowledgement says it is not consent.

**3. Cameras are told as they are.** The notice says the cameras were already there. It says what the practice did afterwards, and it says what the images are not used for. Staff can judge it, which an evasive notice would not let them do.

**4. One exception, stated.** Images are not used to check work, hours or conduct, but an image of a theft or an assault can be used to deal with it. Saying so is better than a blanket promise the practice would break the first time something happened.

**5. A complaint route that skips the owner.** In a three-person practice the owner is the controller, the privacy contact and the incident lead. The notice tells staff they may go to the authority directly, and lets them ask the external adviser to look at a concern about the owner.

**6. Gaps in the notice, with dates.** The access-log retention period is missing. The notice says so and says when it will be fixed. A fake number would be worse.

**7. Same facts, five places.** The camera rules are checked across five documents, and the one small mismatch is written down.

**8. A checklist that follows the data.** A leaver triggers a retention clock. The checklist ties the day someone leaves to the periods in the retention schedule, and to the destruction register at the end.

## 8. Romanian national layer

> **Romania-specific points** (the notice and checklist apply anywhere in the EU, with local retention rules)
>
> - **Monitoring of employees.** Art. 5 of Law no. 190/2018 requires prior, complete and explicit information, consultation of the union or representatives, and storage of no more than 30 days. The notice and the acknowledgement are the information step.
> - **National ID number.** Art. 4 of Law no. 190/2018 sets safeguards for processing the CNP. *Exact wording to be confirmed.*
> - **Employee register and authorities.** Reports go to the labour inspectorate (REGES), the tax authority and the funds. *To be confirmed.*
> - **Retention.** Personnel files follow employment and archiving law, and payroll statements the accounting law (see RET-11 in the retention schedule). *Personnel file period to be confirmed.*
> - **Authority.** Complaints go to ANSPDCP, which the notice names.

## 9. Blank templates

The blank notice follows the structure above. Yellow fields are for you to complete. Delete section 8 if you do not recruit, and delete the completion notes before use.

**How to use it**

1. List the data you really hold about staff, and delete the lines that do not apply.
2. For each purpose, write the basis (contract, law, legitimate interest) and the retention period from your own schedule.
3. Copy the camera section from your own assessment, not from this example. The areas, the retention and the people with access must be identical.
4. Say how you consulted staff, and when. If the cameras were already there, say so.
5. Give the notice to every employee and collaborator, and file the signed acknowledgement with the personnel file.
6. Use the checklist for every joiner and leaver, and review the notice each year.
