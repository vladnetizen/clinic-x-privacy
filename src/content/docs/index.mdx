## What this is

Clinic X is an invented family-medicine practice in Romania: an owner-physician, a nurse, a receptionist and a visiting physician, with about 2,800 patient records. It starts with one good document, a patient notice, and nothing else written down.

Over twelve modules it gets a privacy programme that a practice of that size can actually run: the documents, the registers that show they are used, and blank templates to adapt. Every legal statement shows its source, and anything that was not verified says so.
